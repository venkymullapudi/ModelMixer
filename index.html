What each mix sees

Where each mix looks

Pixel saliency for every method on every task, next to each expert's own map. A high pattern match means the merge reads the same pixels as the expert; a high match with low accuracy is the conflict signature, and a low match with high accuracy means the mix solves the task another way (a Mixture of Experts router, for example). Each map is scaled to its own peak. Click a row to open it in the Lab.

Interpolation path A → B

Walk in a straight line from Expert A’s weights to Expert B’s. A dip in the middle is a loss barrier — it means plain averaging lands in a bad region. Neuron alignment (Git Re-Basin) can flatten it when experts don’t share a base.

Scaling sweep λ

How strongly should task vectors be added back? Task Arithmetic is sensitive to λ; TIES and DARE trade a little peak accuracy for a flatter, more forgiving curve.
