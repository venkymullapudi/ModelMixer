# ModelMixer improvement backlog

Audience: **practitioners picking a merge recipe** for real checkpoints (then exporting to mergekit).
Each improvement loop takes the top unchecked item, ships it (tests green, bundle rebuilt, no console
errors, no horizontal scroll at 375px) and moves it to *Done* with the date.

## How to verify an iteration

- Phone: `python3 scripts/phone_audit.py` should show ✓ (no horizontal overflow) for every view and state.
- Accessibility: keep axe-core at 0 violations on every view (Lab, Arena, Atlas, Export, advisor) and keep every new control labelled and keyboard reachable.

- `node tests/run.js` (CI runs this too). Without Node: `python3 scripts/bundle.py --tests`, then open
  `tests/_browser.html` and expect `N/N passed`.
- `python3 scripts/bundle.py`, then open `dist/modelmixer.html`, exercise the changed feature, and check
  the console for errors.

- 2026-11-22: **AdaMerging objective, take 2, plus a correction.** I measured five objectives × two modes on 12 bundles (Two / Three / Hard + easy / Digits × 3 seeds):
  - **The correction:** on the image-like **Digits** toy, AdaMerging beats Task Arithmetic by about 12 pts (task-wise entropy 96.6 vs 84.6), matching the paper's vision results. The earlier "trails Task Arithmetic" note only held for the 2-D toys.
  - **Kept:** *anchored entropy* (entropy plus a small pull toward λ₀ = 0.3) is never worse than plain entropy in any slice, and **task-wise anchored** has the best overall mean (85.7) and the best Digits mean (96.7), so it is the new default. *Expert-average targets* are the steadiest overall (sd 5.0, worst 76.0) and help the 2-D toys, but cost about 6 pts on Digits, so they are kept as an option.
  - **Dropped:** most-confident-expert labels and entropy-only-where-experts-agree were worse.

  Tests cover that both objectives lower their losses and that anchoring keeps λ at least as close to λ₀ (61 total). axe 0.

- 2026-11-22: **SCE and AdaMerging** (17 methods). SCE selects high-variance entries, weights experts by energy and erases minority signs. Tests: task-wise AdaMerging equals Task Arithmetic with the learned λs exactly, entropy decreases, and SCE selects the requested fraction. At top-k 1 SCE skips the entries where experts agree exactly (17% here). 50 tests.
